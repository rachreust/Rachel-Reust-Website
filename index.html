About

Explore My Background

How I Think, Work, and See the World

My Why Behind Design

Design, for me, is the process of turning that operational mess into a clear path forward. What matters most is helping people move faster, think clearer, and make better decisions through design.

Things I Love About Design

I love the Aha moment a complicated problem begins to reveal. Understanding relationships, mapping, shaping an simplifying Workflows is the part of design I never get tired of. I care deeply about visual craft, but the interface only works when the system beneath it makes sense.

When I’m Not Designing

Military life has taken my family across the world and taught me how to adapt quickly, build community, and stay curious in unfamiliar places. Outside of work, I’m usually horseback riding, painting, volunteering for the military community, exploring with my three boys, or being pulled farther than planned by my husky.

How I Want to Grow as a Designer

I’m focused on growing my product thinking, strategic decision making, and ability to shape systems that create meaningful outcomes at scale. I want to keep moving toward products where the interface is only one layer of the problem: complex data, permissions, handoffs, competing priorities, and real operational consequences.

What I’ve Learned Living Abroad

Living in Japan sharpened my attention to what people understand before a word is translated: hierarchy, iconography, context, sequence, and behavior. It taught me to enter unfamiliar environments with curiosity instead of assumptions, and that has made me a better researcher, collaborator, and designer.
